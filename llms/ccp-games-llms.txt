# CCP Games — EVE Swagger Interface (ESI)

> CCP Games is the studio behind EVE Online, a single-shard massively multiplayer space sandbox. Its public developer API, the EVE Swagger Interface (ESI), exposes ~197 operations across 31 domains (character, corporation, alliance, market, industry, universe, fleets, mail, and more) reading from and writing to the live Tranquility server. ESI is secured with EVE SSO (OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect) using fine-grained per-scope consent and is governed by a free third-party Developer License Agreement.
